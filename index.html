Worked Example · Fall 2025Warmed composites · answers condensed from their handout
From Objective to Question
BO 1
Pre-heating lowers the viscosity of a packable composite and raises monomer conversion when it is cured at that temperature.
BO 2
Heating a conventional composite drops viscosity, but not to flowable levels, unless it is a thermoviscous formulation.
BO 3
Better wetting, lower film thickness, and more stickiness improve adaptation to the internal walls and handling.
BO 4
Dispensers, bench warmers, wax pots, and operatory lights. Cost, heating outside the manufacturer's instructions, and added chair time.
BO 5
Four minutes on the operatory light to reach 64°C, a peak above 80°C, and under a minute to cool back to device temperature.
The quiz question objective 5 produced
How long does it take to raise the resin composite temperature inside a compule to match the temperature of a commercial pre-heating device?
A. 3 minutesB. 10 minutesC. 4 minutesD. 1 minuteE. 7 minutes
The whole class answered this right after the session, and two more of their objectives became questions on Exam #4. The group then took the work further: it is now a continuing education article in Inside Dentistry.

Six Thinking Hats, six seats at the table
Six modes of thinking, mapped onto the people who actually argue about a material. Everyone speaks from a seat, so the disagreement stays productive. Run them inside the presentation or as a ten-minute block right after it.

Do the Homework
Evidence, and where it came from
Aim for the highest level of evidence you can find, then show your work: PICO framing, databases, the search string, how many results, and where what you found sits in the hierarchy.
Explain the search, do not just show it
The most repeated correction last year was a search strategy slide nobody talked through. Say how you went from thousands of hits to the handful you used, and why those. If you found nothing, say so and identify where your information came from instead.
Go past the sales rep
Some of these topics have thin peer-reviewed support. Learn to reach a manufacturer's research team directly for real answers about composition and testing. You will need this in practice, and a chatbot is not a database.
